Nitro Volume Events

Momentum, funded by a reserve, never by printing

When 1-hour volume runs at 3× the trailing 24-hour hourly average, a 30-minute Nitro window opens and buy-side cashback pays 2×. The bonus comes from the Nitro reserve, which collects 0.05% of every trade during calm hours. It is never minted and never taken from the other lanes. When the reserve is empty, the window pays 1×. The sell tax never changes, so there is no reason to churn.

Questions

Straight answers

Is 2% really the whole tax?

Yes. 2% on buys and 2% on sells, taken in $GASMAX at transfer. There is no setter function, so it cannot be raised, and no exemption list, so it cannot be dodged by insiders.

If the team takes no cut, how do they get paid?

From the swap fees that protocol-owned liquidity earns on the DEX, 0.3% of pair volume. That is the only team income, it grows with the same volume everyone else earns from, and it is reported on this page.

Can liquidity be pulled?

No. LP tokens from the launch pool are burned, and the 0.2% lane adds liquidity that is burned the same way. Depth can only increase.

Is the yield inflationary?

No tokens are ever minted. Every rebate and every Pit Stop payout is ETH bought with the tax on real trades. If volume drops, payouts drop. That is the honest version of yield.

Which chain?

Base at launch, so rebates are paid in ETH and gas is cheap enough that small traders come out ahead. Other chains follow once the model has run for a few epochs.
